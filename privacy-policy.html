SocialFlow AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

SocialFlow AI may collect account information, restaurant information, connected social account details, post content, scheduling details, and OAuth tokens required for authorized social media integrations.

How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate users, connect social accounts, publish and schedule content, and provide supported analytics.

Data Sharing

SocialFlow AI does not sell personal data. Data is shared with connected social platforms only when required to perform actions requested by users.

Security

OAuth credentials and access tokens are handled by the backend and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SocialFlow AI account and associated data.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the SocialFlow AI project administrator.
